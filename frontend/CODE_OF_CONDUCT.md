# Code of conduct

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Discuss the code and the problem. Give actionable feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and allow people time to respond. Contributors have different experience levels and may use different tools or languages.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual content directed at others, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [dhibi.ywsf@gmail.com](mailto:dhibi.ywsf@gmail.com). Include links or context needed to understand what happened. Do not post another person's private information in a public report.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to keep project spaces usable. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled privately where possible.
